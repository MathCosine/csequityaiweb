What you’ll see

The model isn’t the hard part. The system around it is.

An AI on its own is like a brilliant intern who never checks their work. Engineers build a harness around it: a system that tests every answer and sends mistakes back to be fixed.

  1. 1
    TryThe AI writes a program for a problem it has never seen.
  2. 2
    CheckWe run it against hidden tests. It passes the easy ones and trips on a tricky one.
  3. 3
    FixWe feed the failure back. The AI reads its mistake and corrects its own code.

After the bootcamp

Then build one yourself in the Agentic AI Bootcamp.

An 8-week online course where you build the harness that makes an AI agent work, going from a one-line script to an AI that solves problems on its own. It’s free, runs on any laptop, and you finish with a project you can show. The registration form asks whether you’re interested, so you don’t need to sign up twice.

Course outline
Week 1
The map: what you’ll build and how agents work
Weeks 2–3
Get the AI to write code, then check it safely
Week 4
The feedback loop: it learns from its mistakes
Weeks 5–6
Make it better: many tries, targeted fixes
Weeks 7–8
Assemble your harness and see what’s next
