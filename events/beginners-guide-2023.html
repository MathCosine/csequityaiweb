2023 · A Beginner's Guide to AI & Android Development

Friday, February 3, 2023, 7:00 PM — Wednesday, June 21, 2023, 8:00 PM

Workshop Archive

Highlights from classes, March through May 2023.

As we wrapped up this 8-week course designed for students with no prior experience in computer science, we reflected on an incredible journey. With a diverse group of 16 students — 10 high school sophomores, 4 juniors, 1 senior, and a college senior majoring in Information Engineering — we aimed to ignite a passion for technology and programming.

Highlights of the course

Engaging guest speakers

Industry professionals shared their experiences and insights, inspiring students and grounding the concepts in real-world context. We are immensely grateful to all our guest speakers.

Course structure

Segment 1 · Android application modification

  • Objective: Modify the TensorFlow Lite Pose Estimation Android demo
  • Skills developed: Programming, machine learning, and mobile app development
  • Outcome: Students built an AI-powered Android pose classifier using Human Pose Classification with MoveNet and TensorFlow Lite

Segment 2 · Cloud-based Android system development

  • Objective: Develop Android systems on Google Cloud Platform
  • Skills developed: Setting up virtual machines, building and modifying Android systems, and cloud development

Looking ahead

The skills these students acquired form a strong foundation for their journeys in technology. Thank you to everyone who participated — especially our guest speakers, whose insights made a lasting impact.
