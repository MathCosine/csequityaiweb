Android System Development on Cloud — Demo Day

Friday, December 3, 2021 · 7:00–10:00 PM

The original code lab poster — Oct 15, 22, 29 & Nov 6, 2021, 5:30–6:30 PM PDT on Google Meet.

This was the conclusion of our first code lab — four weeks through the basics of cloud computing and Android, and the beginning of everything CS Equity AI has built since. Big thanks to Mei-Mei for connecting us with the students and inviting us to commentate on such a special event.

To all the students, whether you were on stage presenting or listening in: we wish, from the bottom of our hearts, that you find a career you're passionate about — be it computer science or not.

And to the students who came up on stage and walked us through their debugging process live: we wouldn't be here without you.
