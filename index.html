01 — The path

A year-long pipeline from first lesson to founding team

Not a lecture series. Each level ends with something real: a certificate, a working AI application, a pitch in front of investors.

i.ii.iii.
Level one — Capability

Learn the essentials

Self-paced AI and CS fundamentals on the OpenImpact Lab, our free online learning platform. Finishing the Foundation course is what qualifies you for the bootcamp — and it’s open right now.

  • Everything runs in the browser — no setup, no installs
  • Hands-on labs with modern AI tooling
  • Foundation Certificate on completion
Start level one →
Level two — Creation

Build in the bootcamp

Weekly live sessions with coaches from Google and academia. You aren’t studying AI systems — you’re shipping one, from the ground up, with two rounds of professional rehearsal before demo day.

  • Live coaching in small teams
  • Two rounds of professional presentation rehearsal
  • Ship a working “Hello World” AI system & earn your second certificate
Level three — Capital

Launch a startup

The live-fire exercise. Form a founding team of three to five, build an MVP from your own idea or the “Googler Wishlist,” and pitch it directly to angel investors.

  • Weekly startup coaching
  • Demo a real Minimum Viable Product
  • Pitch day with angel investors
