01 — The path

A year-long pipeline from first lesson to founding team

Not a lecture series. Each level ends with something real: a certificate, a working AI application, a pitch in front of investors.

iCapability

Learn the essentials

Self-paced AI and CS fundamentals on the OpenImpact Lab, our free online learning platform. Finishing the Foundation course is what qualifies you for the bootcamp.

  • Everything runs in the browser — no setup
  • Hands-on labs with modern AI tooling
  • Foundation Certificate on completion
iiCreation

Build in the bootcamp

Weekly live sessions with coaches from Google and academia. You leave with a functioning AI system you built yourself — and a second certificate.

  • Live coaching, small teams
  • Two rounds of professional rehearsal
  • Ship a working "Hello World" AI system
iiiCapital

Launch a startup

Form a founding team of three to five, build an MVP from your own idea or the "Googler Wishlist," and pitch it directly to angel investors.

  • Weekly startup coaching
  • Demo a real Minimum Viable Product
  • Pitch day with angel investors
