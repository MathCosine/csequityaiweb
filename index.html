The Path

From learner to 1-person unicorn founder

Our year-long pipeline takes ambitious students from AI fundamentals to a working product — in three levels.

Level 1 · Capability

Gain Essential Skills

Master the tools of the trade at the OpenImpact Lab — our free, self-paced online learning platform. Earn your Foundation Certificate to prove you're ready for the heavy lifting.

  • Self-paced AI & CS fundamentals, entirely in the browser
  • Hands-on labs with modern AI tooling
  • Foundation Certificate on completion
1
Level 2 · Creation

The Bootcamp

An intensive online bootcamp where you go from student to creator — building a functioning AI application from the ground up, with coaching from industry professionals.

  • Weekly live sessions with coaches from Google & academia
  • Two rounds of professional rehearsal coaching
  • Ship a working "Hello World" AI system & earn your second certificate
2
Level 3 · Capital

Launch an AI-Native Startup

The live-fire exercise. Form a founding team of 3–5, build an MVP from your own vision or the "Googler Wishlist," and pitch directly to angel investors.

  • Realistic startup training with weekly coaching
  • Build and demo a real Minimum Viable Product
  • Pitch to Angels and get ready for take-off
3
