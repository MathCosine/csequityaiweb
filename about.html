About Us

A registered 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to inclusive AI education.

CS Equity AI is committed to the principle of computational social equity — making sure the benefits of artificial intelligence are accessible and fair across all communities.

Our Mission

Empowering diverse leaders with accessible AI education

Our mission is to drive computational social equity by empowering the next generation of diverse leaders with accessible Artificial Intelligence education and advanced Computer Science skills.

Since initiating our free community services in 2021, and formally forming as a nonprofit in 2024, we leverage state-of-the-art technology and global collaboration to ensure students — regardless of background or geographic location — can apply ethical AI solutions to solve real-world problems and contribute meaningfully to their communities worldwide.

At a glance

  • Verified 501(c)(3) Public Charity — EIN 99-3463829
  • Free community programs since 2021; formally incorporated 2024
  • Students across the United States, Taiwan & Hong Kong
  • Coaches & mentors from Google and leading universities
  • 100% online, cloud-based classrooms — all you need is a browser
How We Work

Our model for social equity

Three core, scalable strategies that turn educational access into long-term career success.

🎓

Accessible & Cutting-Edge Education

We deliver zero-cost, high-quality CS education focused on cloud computing, data analytics, and ethical AI — using a scalable cloud-based learning platform so students in underserved areas get the same access as everyone else.

🛤️

Strategic Pathways to Industry

We forge partnerships with educational institutions and leading technology companies, and facilitate mentorship, networking, and demo days that build direct career pathways for our students.

⚖️

Championing Equity & Inclusion

We champion diverse, inclusive environments across the technology sector and equip graduates with a commitment to ethical AI — driving computational social equity from within the industry.
