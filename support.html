Support Us

Every dollar funds a seat that costs a student nothing.

Gifts to CS EQUITY AI are tax-deductible — we're a verified 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 99-3463829). Donations fund scholarships, mentorship, and the cloud infrastructure our free programs run on.

Where it goes

Your investment, in three lines

i

Accessible, cutting-edge education

Zero-cost, cloud-based education in ethical AI and data science fundamentals, delivered through the OpenImpact Lab.

ii

Pathways to industry

Mentorship from professionals at Google and leading universities, cultivating ethical leaders who drive computational social equity.

iii

Equity & inclusion

Industry partnerships that unlock high-value career opportunities for students regardless of background or location.

How to give

Two ways, both simple

Corporate matching

Benevity

CS Equity AI is a verified nonprofit partner with major workplace giving platforms, including Benevity. If your employer matches, your gift can be doubled or tripled at no extra cost to you.

  1. Log in to your company's employee giving or CSR portal.
  2. Search for "CS Equity AI" or EIN 99-3463829.
  3. Submit your match request — your employer handles the rest.
