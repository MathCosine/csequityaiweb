Vibe Coding Happy Hour — Pilot Run

Two hours that took 8–14 year olds from "I've never written code" to a game they built themselves with the Gemini CLI. Run in person at a Google office in Mountain View with volunteer engineer coaches, teams of about five, and a show & tell at the end. The full curriculum is open source — and the program is still open to schools.
