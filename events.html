2026May 17
2-hour workshop

Vibe Coding Happy Hour — Pilot Run

Two hours that took 8–14 year olds from “I’ve never written code” to a game they built themselves with the Gemini CLI. Run in person at a Google office in Mountain View with volunteer engineer coaches, teams of about five, and a show & tell at the end. The full curriculum is open source, and schools can still request a session.

The Course of Making AI Tutor for [X]

Fifty university students from Taiwan, HK & the US built their own AI Tutors in teams of five, each coached by a Googler. The course was inspired by Harvard’s Teaching CS50 with AI and built on Google Cloud with the Gemini API.
